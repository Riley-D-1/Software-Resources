How a Brain Machine Learns

How a brain machine learns

Neural networks, in six pictures.

Page 1

Your brain is full of tiny helpers.

They pass little sparks to each other. That's how you think.

Page 2

A computer copies the idea.

Lots of dots, joined by lines, in rows. That's a neural network.

Page 3

Picture in. Answer out.

Show it a picture. The sparks hop from row to row until one answer lights up.

Page 4

Every line has a volume knob.

Some lines whisper. Some lines shout. The knobs decide the answer.

Page 5

It learns by guessing wrong.

Wrong? Turn the knobs a tiny bit. Try again. Again. Again.

Sure:
Guess: Dog? ✗
Page 6

Lots of practice makes it smart.

After seeing heaps of examples, the knobs are just right. Now it guesses well, even on new pictures.

That's it. Dots, lines, knobs, and lots of practice.
