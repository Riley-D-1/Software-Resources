Chrome DevTools for Beginners

Every web page has a secret toolbox.

Chrome DevTools lets you look inside any page: how it's built, what it's saying, and what it downloaded. Builders use it to find and fix problems.

How to open it

Right-click anythingthen pick Inspect
WindowsF12 or Ctrl Shift I
MacCmd Option I

What's in the toolbox

Elements

The page's building blocks. Point at a part to see what it's made of, then change its colour or words.

like: the page's skeleton

Console

Where the page leaves notes. Mistakes show up in red. You can type commands to it, too.

like: a walkie-talkie to the page

Network

Every picture and file the page fetched, how big it was, and how long it took to arrive.

like: watching delivery trucks

Sources

The page's instructions. You can pause them on any line to see what's happening, step by step.

like: pausing a recipe mid-way

Performance

A stopwatch for the page. It shows which part makes things slow.

like: timing a race

Application

What the site saved on your computer, like cookies and remembered settings.

like: the site's backpack

Device mode

Pretend your computer is a phone or tablet to see how the page fits.

the little phone icon, top left

Lighthouse

A report card. It grades the page on speed and ease of use, out of 100.

like: a school report

You can't break anything

Changes only happen on your screen. Refresh the page and it goes back to normal. Nobody else sees them.
